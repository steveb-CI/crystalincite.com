Why it's different

The answer isn't generated.
It's assembled from evidence.

General-purpose AI assistants can state things that aren't true, with complete confidence. Crystal Incite is built differently: reasoning is deterministic, and a language model is used only to format the final response — never to decide what's true.◆This is enforced in the architecture, not promised in marketing: the reasoning path calls no language model.

Grounded in your documents

Answers come from what your company has actually written down — specs, plans, logs, records. Nothing is pulled from the open internet or a model's memory.

Deterministic reasoning

The same question over the same documents gives the same answer. No language model sits in the reasoning path, so there's nothing to guess, drift, or invent.

The trail comes attached

Every claim links back to the exact evidence it was built from. When someone asks "how do you know?", the answer is a click, not a shrug.

How it works

From documents to defensible answers

  1. 1

    Connect your documents

    Crystal Incite works from the documents your team already keeps — no migration, no re-writing your records into someone else's format.

  2. 2

    Evidence is extracted

    The system identifies the statements, figures, and tables inside your documents and keeps a precise record of where each one came from.

  3. 3

    Claims are assembled

    When you ask a question, claims are built from that evidence by deterministic reasoning — rules, not a model's intuition.

  4. 4

    The answer carries its trail

    You get a plain-language answer where every sentence can be traced back to the document, page, and passage that supports it.

Built for work that faces auditors, inspectors, regulators, and customers — where a wrong answer isn't an inconvenience, it's an event.
